# Changelog

All notable changes to this package are documented in this file.

## [0.0.1] - 2026-11-01

### Added
- Automatic detection of color regions in atlas textures, with a tolerance setting and a filter for regions the model uses.
- Region selection from the Scene view, the atlas image and color swatches.
- Live preview that leaves materials, textures and scenes untouched until saved.
- Built-in palettes and custom palette assets, with brightness mapping and shuffling.
- Random variants with hue shift and random colors.
- Saving as a new texture and material, or over the source texture.
